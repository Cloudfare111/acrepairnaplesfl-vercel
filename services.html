Preventative Care

24-Point Precision AC Maintenance & Tune-Up

Southwest Florida's hot, humid climate forces air conditioners to run up to 3,000 hours each year. Without regular maintenance, efficiency drops by 5% to 15% annually, electric bills climb, and sudden failures occur. Our 24-point seasonal tune-up restores factory efficiency and protects equipment longevity.

  • Coil Cleaning & Chemical Sanitizing: Removing dust, salt, and bio-growth from evaporator and condenser fins to restore heat transfer.
  • Refrigerant Pressure Calibration: Verifying subcooling and superheat to ensure the compressor operates within safe temperature limits.
  • Condensate Line Flush & Tablet Treatment: Preventing algae clogs that trigger emergency float switch shutdowns and water leaks.

HVAC FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

Clear, honest answers about diagnostic appointments, pricing, warranties, and emergency dispatch.

Do you service all air conditioning brands in Naples?
Yes. Our EPA-certified technicians are trained and equipped to service all major residential and light commercial HVAC brands, including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, York, Goodman, Amana, and American Standard.
What is included in an AC diagnostic service call?
Our diagnostic inspection tests electrical capacitors and contactors, measures refrigerant line temperatures and operating pressures, inspects the indoor evaporator and outdoor condenser coils, checks the blower motor amperage, and verifies the condensate drain line.
Are emergency cooling services available on weekends in Naples?
Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency dispatch 365 days a year across Naples and Collier County. If your AC breaks down in extreme summer heat or on a holiday weekend, our on-call technicians respond immediately.
