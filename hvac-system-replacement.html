Homeowner Guide

When Should You Replace Your AC in Naples?

We believe in honest recommendations. We never push replacements when a simple repair makes economic sense. However, replacement is typically the smarter financial decision when:

1. Age Exceeds 10 to 12 Years

In northern states, air conditioners last 15–20 years. In coastal Florida, where systems run 3,000+ hours annually in humid, salty air, 10–12 years is the typical operational lifespan. Past 10 years, major parts face fatigue failure.

2. Obsolete R-22 Freon

If your older system was manufactured prior to 2010 and uses phased-out R-22 refrigerant, fixing a leak can cost $800 to $1,800 just for the gas. Upgrading to modern R-410A / R-454B systems ends the endless refrigerant refill trap.

3. The 5,000 Rule

Multiply the age of your equipment by the repair cost. If the total exceeds $5,000 (e.g., an 11-year-old unit needing a $650 repair = $7,150), the system has reached the point of diminishing returns and is best replaced.

Florida Climate Technology

Why Variable-Speed Inverters Excel in Naples Homes

Older single-stage air conditioners blast on at 100% capacity and shut off quickly. In humid Naples, this "short-cycling" cools the air without removing indoor humidity, leaving your home cold and clammy.

•

Continuous Dehumidification

Variable-speed compressors ramp down to 30%–40% capacity, running continuously at low speeds to steadily wring moisture from indoor air, keeping relative humidity at a healthy 45%–50%.

•

30% to 50% Lower FPL Power Bills

Eliminating hard compressor starts and running at ultra-efficient partial loads significantly cuts energy consumption compared to older 10–12 SEER systems.

•

Whisper-Quiet Sound Levels

Operating as low as 55 decibels—about the sound of quiet rainfall—you will barely hear your outdoor unit running outside your lanai or patio.

Replacement FAQ

Frequently Asked HVAC Replacement Questions

Transparent answers about new system sizing, timelines, and warranties.

When should I replace my air conditioner instead of repairing it in Naples?
In Florida, HVAC systems typically last 10 to 12 years due to heavy operating hours and coastal salt exposure. If your system is over 10 years old, relies on obsolete R-22 refrigerant, has a cracked coil or burned-out compressor, or requires repairs exceeding $1,000, investing in a new high-efficiency SEER2 unit delivers lower energy bills and reliable comfort.
How long does a new HVAC system installation take?
Most residential replacements in Naples are completed within a single day (typically 6 to 8 hours). Our installation crew removes the old indoor and outdoor units, updates electrical disconnects and refrigeration lines, installs safety float switches, and balances airflow before evening.
What warranty comes with a new HVAC system in Naples, FL?
Our installations feature up to a 10-year manufacturer warranty on major components (compressor and coils) along with our company labor and workmanship guarantees.
