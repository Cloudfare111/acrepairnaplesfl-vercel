Privacy Policy

How Premium AC Solutions protects and respects your personal information when you visit our website or request HVAC services in Naples, FL.

Last Updated: January 2026

At Premium AC Solutions, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal information we collect, how it is used, and the security measures we maintain when you visit our website or use our air conditioning repair, maintenance, and installation services in Naples, Florida.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us via telephone or fill out our online dispatch and quote request forms, we collect the personal information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name and contact phone number
  • Your physical service address or neighborhood in Collier County
  • Your email address for service invoices and confirmations
  • Details concerning your HVAC equipment, service history, and cooling symptoms

We also automatically collect standard, non-personally identifiable technical telemetry when you browse our site, such as browser type, operating system, pages visited, and referring URLs to ensure optimal website performance and mobile usability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected information solely to provide professional residential and light commercial HVAC services:

  • Scheduling and dispatching certified technicians to your property
  • Providing accurate diagnostic estimates, invoices, and equipment warranty registration
  • Communicating appointment arrival windows and technician status
  • Responding directly to customer service inquiries and emergency cooling calls

3. Data Protection & Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties or marketing lists. We only share information with trusted operational partners when necessary to deliver our services (such as equipment manufacturers for processing warranty claims, or secure communication platforms for scheduling SMS appointment updates).

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website utilizes standard session cookies to remember preferences, analyze aggregate traffic patterns via Google Analytics, and ensure form security. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time; however, certain site features may have reduced functionality.

5. Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard technical and physical controls, including SSL/TLS encryption for all web transmission, to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
