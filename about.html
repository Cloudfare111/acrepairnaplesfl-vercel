Our Story

Dedicated to Keeping Naples Comfortable Year-Round

Naples has flourished dramatically from its early pioneer roots in the late 19th century into one of Florida's premier coastal communities. From the deepwater canals of Port Royal and Aqualane Shores to the tranquil golf communities of North and East Naples, quality of life in Southwest Florida depends fundamentally on reliable indoor climate control.

With summer temperatures consistently surpassing 92°F and relative humidity levels exceeding 85%, air conditioning systems in Collier County endure exceptional mechanical stress. Salt spray along Gulf coastlines accelerates exterior condenser coil corrosion, while non-stop condensation inside dark attic ductwork creates rapid algae buildup in drain pans.

At Premium AC Solutions, we were established on a simple, refreshing philosophy: provide meticulous technical diagnostics, honest advice, and transparent pricing without predatory sales tactics or unnecessary system condemnation. When an AC unit breaks down, we focus first on accurate component-level repair—replacing worn capacitors, clearing blocked condensate traps, and repairing refrigerant leaks before ever recommending a costly replacement.

How We Work

Our 4 Core Commitments to Collier County Homeowners

We hold ourselves to strict trade standards so you always know what to expect when a technician arrives at your door.

01

Accurate Diagnoses & No Upselling

Our technicians are not commission-driven salespeople. We explain the exact component failure with verifiable multimeter and gauge readings, present clear repair options, and never push replacement unless the unit is mechanically irreparable or cost-prohibitive.

02

Coastal Heat & Salt Air Mastery

Naples' proximity to the Gulf of Mexico demands specialized knowledge. We understand electrolytic corrosion on copper-aluminum coils, properly treat condensate traps prone to subtropical algae blooms, and ensure systems maintain sub-55% indoor humidity.

03

Punctual Arrival & Stocked Trucks

We respect your schedule and know that waiting in a sweltering home is unbearable. Our technicians arrive within confirmed arrival windows with fully equipped vans carrying standard universal capacitors, contactors, fan motors, and Safe-T-Switches for immediate single-visit resolution.

04

Written Workmanship Guarantees

We stand behind every repair and replacement with written warranties on both parts and labor. If an issue recurs after our visit, we return promptly to make it right under our comprehensive service guarantee.
