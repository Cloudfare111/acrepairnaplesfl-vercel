Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding scheduling, diagnostics, and service coverage across Naples.

How quickly can a technician respond to an AC emergency in Naples? +
For complete cooling breakdowns during summer heat, our on-call technicians prioritize same-day dispatch. Because our dispatch facility is centrally located on Enterprise Avenue, service vans are positioned throughout Collier County for rapid arrival.
Do you service all air conditioning brands and models? +
Yes. Our technicians carry EPA Universal Section 608 certifications and have extensive factory training on all major HVAC brands, including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, York, Goodman, Amana, American Standard, and Daikin.
What should I do while waiting for the technician to arrive? +
If your indoor evaporator coil is frozen or if you see ice forming along the copper suction line, turn the thermostat system setting from COOL to OFF, but leave the FAN switch set to ON. This allows the coil to thaw safely without burning out the compressor motor. If water is backing up in the drain pan, place towels down and turn the system off immediately.
Are estimates on new HVAC replacements free? +
Yes. We provide complimentary, zero-pressure in-home estimates for complete heat pump and air conditioner replacements. We calculate proper Manual J cooling loads, inspect your existing ductwork, and present multiple SEER2 efficiency tiers tailored to your budget.
